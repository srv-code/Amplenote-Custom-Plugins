[^1]: [much safer]()

    As it only adds/updates on a singular section and doesn't touch the rest of the note content like similar other TOC plugins.

[^2]: [reliable]()

    As it checks for the duplicate and offending (unsupported) section titles and is also fail-safe i.e. aborts the operation asking the user to manually fix them to avoid broken linkages. These behaviors are also configurable.

[^3]: [superior]()

    It looks out for several other options as per the authors personal requirements and preserves the header levels by default. All the behaviors are configurable and works on fallbacks too.
